## The Pipeline

VOLTA covers concept art through delivery in a single CLI — 12
pipeline stages, hybrid AI/DCC, built for one-person or small-team
production sprints.

**Repository:** [github.com/andrewlrose/volta-pipeline](https://github.com/andrewlrose/volta-pipeline)

**Toolchain:**
ComfyUI · Flux.1 · Stable Diffusion · IP-Adapter · LoRA training ·
Meshy · ZRemesher · Substance Painter · AccuRig · MotionBuilder HIK ·
Cascadeur · Unreal Engine 5 · Movie Render Queue · DaVinci Resolve ·
FFmpeg · ElevenLabs
